# Data Analytics Capstone Project: Retail & E-Commerce Insights (Option A)

## Project Overview
This repository contains the final capstone project for the **Python Programming Data Analyst Professional Certificate**. The primary objective of this project is to analyze store performance, evaluate revenue drivers, and provide actionable business recommendations based on an integrated e-commerce relational dataset.

* **Business Context:** E-commerce sales analysis and customer segmentation.
* **Scope:** Integrated 7 relational datasets (`customers`, `orders`, `order_items`, `products`, `payments`, `shipments`, and `returns`).
* **Environment:** Python (Pandas, Seaborn, Matplotlib) executed via Google Colab.

---

## Executive Summary
* Integrated seven distinct relational e-commerce datasets into a unified, analysis-ready master fact table.
* Performed rigorous data cleaning, handling null values, eliminating duplicate entries, and standardizing product categories.
* Parsed mixed date formats and cleaned currency fields to ensure absolute numerical and chronological integrity.
* Conducted exploratory data analysis (EDA) to evaluate total revenue, transaction counts, and average order values (AOV).
* Discovered that a concentrated tier of top-performing merchandise categories drives the majority of gross sales.
* Identified distinct regional demand imbalances, showing that metropolitan hubs heavily outperform regional zones.
* Highlighted the increased basket size and lifetime value of repeat shoppers compared to one-time buyers.
* Visualized performance metrics through five distinct analytical charts to support data-driven decision-making.
* Provided strategic, actionable recommendations focused on regional marketing and inventory risk mitigation.

## Key Insights & Recommendations

### **Insights**
* **Category Concentration:** Over 60% of total revenue is generated by the top three merchandise categories.
* **Regional Disparities:** Urban areas account for the highest volume of transactions and larger basket sizes.
* **Customer Value:** Repeat customers consistently exhibit higher average spending power, validating retention efforts.

### **Recommendations**
1. **Targeted Regional Campaigns:** Deploy localized digital promotions and optimized shipping incentives to capture underperforming geographic segments.
2. **Inventory Optimization:** Prioritize safety stock levels and streamline vendor lead times for the top 10 revenue-generating products to prevent stockouts.
